Hi, I'm Prof. Antonio Mancuso, and I teach Computer Science, TPSI, and Systems and Networks to upper-secondary computer science students at Istituto Internazionale E.Agnelli in Turin (Italy).

![profmancusoa](profmancusoa.jpg)

This blog is where I share information, articles, and tips about Linux and the world of computing with students and enthusiasts alike.
I know a traditional blog isn't the go-to format for younger generations, but I believe the information here holds up better over time than YouTube videos do. On top of that, a blog is useful to its author too, not just its readers.
I also have a [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@profmancusoa) and a [GitHub account](https://github.com/profmancusoa).
If you're into computer science and my articles caught your interest, feel free to jump into the comments.

**Happy reading!!!!**
